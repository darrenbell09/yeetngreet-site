Docs

YeetnGreet helps MSPs and IT teams onboard and offboard Microsoft 365 users from a Windows PC — without sending customer tenant data to our cloud.

Install · Plans · Offboard · Onboard · More you can do

Install

Download YeetnGreet for Windows from Releases. It is a single app (WebView2). Run it, sign in to the customer tenant you manage, and you are ready to work.

Microsoft Graph runs on your technician’s PC. We only use the cloud for your YeetnGreet license and billing — not mail, files, or user content. Updates are available from inside the app.

Plans

Pricing is per managed tenant, not per seat.

  • Community — free for light use (1 tenant, a small monthly job allowance, and a license-report preview)
  • Team — $39/mo or $390/yr — one tenant, unlimited jobs, full reports
  • MSP — $69/mo or $690/yr — five tenants included, then $12 per extra tenant per month
  • Founding beta — $349/yr for early MSP customers (limited slots) — reserve a slot

You activate your plan in the app. Website checkout is coming; founding interest is collected on the homepage form today.

Offboard

Offboard one person or many (list or CSV). Use templates for handoff, checklist, and shared settings. Preview what will run, confirm deliberately, then go. You can also schedule a job to run later on that same PC.

Onboard

Create or enable a user, require a password change, and optionally assign licenses, groups, and a manager. Templates and CSV cover repeatable or bulk work. Confirm before anything Live runs — same careful path as offboard, including optional scheduling.

More you can do

  • Switch between the tenants you manage
  • License audit, unused seats, and what-if reports — on demand or on a schedule
  • Send reports by email (Microsoft Graph) or to Teams, Slack, or a webhook
  • Optional hybrid Active Directory support per tenant when you need on-prem identity with Entra
