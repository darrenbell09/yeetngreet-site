Refund Policy

YeetnGreet offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on your first purchase.

30-day money-back guarantee

If YeetnGreet isn’t right for you, ask for a refund within 30 days of your first purchase of a paid plan, monthly or annual, and we will refund that purchase in full.

After 30 days

After 30 days, payments are non-refundable. You can cancel anytime. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep access until the end of the term you have paid for.

Renewals and band changes

  • Renewal payments are non-refundable.
  • Charges for moving to a higher licensed-user band are non-refundable.
  • On annual plans, a move to a lower band takes effect at your next renewal, with no refund for the current term.

How to ask for a refund

Look up your order at paddle.net with the email you used at checkout, or email support@yeetngreet.app with your order details. Paddle, our Merchant of Record, processes all refunds.

Paddle

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.

This policy doesn’t limit any rights you have under the consumer law where you live. See also our Terms of Service.
