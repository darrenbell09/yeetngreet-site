Privacy

YeetnGreet performs Microsoft Graph and Exchange operations on the technician workstation against the customer tenant you select.

YeetnGreet cloud services (when enabled) store license and billing metadata only (account email, plan, expiry, tenant/seat caps). We do not receive or retain customer user profiles, mail, files, or activity logs.

Local logs and templates remain on your device under AppData / Documents unless you choose to store them elsewhere.
