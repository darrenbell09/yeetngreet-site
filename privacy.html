Privacy

YeetnGreet is built so customer Microsoft 365 data stays between your technicians and the tenant they are working in—not in our cloud.

Where the work happens

Onboarding, offboarding, reporting, and Graph or Exchange actions run on the technician’s Windows PC. The app signs into the customer tenant you select and talks to Microsoft from that machine.

What we receive

When cloud licensing is enabled, we store only what we need to run your YeetnGreet account: things like account email, plan, expiry, and managed-tenant limits.

We do not receive customer user profiles, mail, files, mailbox activity, or other tenant content.

What stays on your side

Templates, local logs, and related working files remain on your device (typically under AppData or Documents) unless you choose to keep them somewhere else.
