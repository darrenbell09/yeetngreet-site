Privacy

YeetnGreet is built so Microsoft 365 data stays between your technicians and the tenant they are working in, not in our cloud.

Where the work happens

Onboarding, offboarding, reporting, and Graph or Exchange actions run on the technician’s Windows PC. The app signs into the tenant you select and talks to Microsoft from that machine.

What we receive

When cloud licensing is enabled, we store what we need to run your YeetnGreet account: account email, plan, expiry, and licensed-user limits.

When you use account roles and shared company templates, we also store the operational configuration you choose to sync: operator roster (Microsoft account UPN/OID and admin/tech role) and company onboard/offboard templates (action presets and group/SKU references).

We do not receive user profiles, mail, files, mailbox activity, or other tenant content from Graph jobs.

Account identity vs tenant Graph

YeetnGreet roles bind to each technician’s Microsoft work account on your YeetnGreet account. The Graph session used to change a directory is separate and stays between your PC and that tenant.

What stays on your side

Local template cache, logs, and related working files remain on your device (typically under AppData or Documents) unless you choose to keep them somewhere else. Published company templates are mirrored to your license account so every tech PC can sync them.
