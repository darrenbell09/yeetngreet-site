YeetnGreet shows where Microsoft 365 spend is leaking, then gives IT a controlled path to fix it—and keep it fixed.

License reporting

See the waste before you renew.

License health reports surface unused prepaid seats, licenses on disabled accounts, and inactive licensed users with no mail activity—the waste that is hardest to spot in the admin center.

  • Per-SKU unused seats and disabled-assigned counts
  • Inactive licensed users (no mail send/receive/read in 90 days)
  • Monthly savings from your unit costs, with named users to act on

Consistent onboarding

Every new hire lands the same way.

Admins define required properties and provisioning rules once. Technicians follow the template—no improvising fields, groups, or licenses at the desk.

  • Admin-controlled required properties
  • Template-driven groups and license assignment
  • Repeatable results across every joiner

Reliable offboarding

Offboard fast—without leaving decisions to chance.

Preset offboarding templates lock the steps: sign-in blocked, licenses removed, mailbox and OneDrive handled the way you decided—not the way a busy tech remembers that day.

  • Preset templates so decisions stay with IT leadership
  • Fast, repeatable remediation when someone leaves
  • Ongoing maintenance that keeps licenses off idle accounts

Built for

Internal IT leaders, multi-tenant ready.

First release is cloud-only Microsoft 365 / Entra. Manage one organization or several tenants from the same toolkit—visibility when licenses waste money, and a consistent method to remediate.
