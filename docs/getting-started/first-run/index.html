    First run¶

    After install, walk through a low-risk path: pick a tenant, confirm it's the right one, then preview before anything runs.

    Checklist¶

    1. Launch YeetnGreet on your PC
    2. Sign in to your Microsoft 365 tenant on Tenants. Admin consent must already be granted in that tenant. See Admin permissions and consent
    3. Confirm the tenant name and domain are the ones you expect
    4. Open Offboard or Onboard and click Preview before you confirm
    5. Optionally save a template if this tenant has a standard checklist

    Preview lists every planned action and changes nothing.

    Confirm the tenant

    Say the organization name out loud before you confirm. Running a job in the wrong tenant is expensive. The tenant picker at the top of the left rail shows which tenant every job runs in. See Multi-tenant.

    Suggested first job¶

    Goal Path
    Practice offboard flow Offboard with a test user or dry-run style preview
    Practice onboard flow Onboard creating a clearly labeled test account
    See license value License audit on a tenant you know well

    Where local files live¶

    Working files (templates, local logs, and related data) stay on your PC, typically under AppData or Documents — unless you choose another location. They are not uploaded to YeetnGreet’s cloud as job payloads.
