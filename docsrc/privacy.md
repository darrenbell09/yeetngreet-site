# Privacy

YeetnGreet is built so your Microsoft 365 data stays between your IT team’s PCs and your own tenant — not in our cloud.

## Short version

| Topic | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| Where work runs | Onboarding, offboarding, reporting, and Graph actions run on **your IT team’s Windows PC** |
| What we receive | License/billing metadata for your YeetnGreet account (when cloud licensing is enabled) |
| What we do not receive | Your users’ profiles, mail, files, mailbox activity, or other tenant content |
| Local files | Templates and working files stay on your device unless you move them |
